人格图鉴 · 16 型人格测试 · 使用说明与常见问题

本应用基于荣格心理类型理论，用 60 道标准题或 24 道快速题测出你的 16 型人格，并给出四个维度的偏向强度、类型画像与相处建议。

所有答题与结果只保存在本机：无需注册、无需登录，也没有服务器。

快速上手

  1. 选择版本：首次推荐「标准版 60 题」（约 8 分钟），结果更稳定；时间紧就用「快速版 24 题」（约 3 分钟）。
  2. 逐题作答：凭第一反应选择更接近的一项，不要在单题上纠结太久，也不要按“理想中的自己”作答。答题中途可退出，已完成部分不会保存。
  3. 查看结果：提交后立即给出四字母类型、各维度百分比与完整解读。
  4. 深入阅读：结果页可进入该类型的详情页，也可随时从首页进入「16 型图鉴」浏览全部类型。
  5. 回看记录：每次结果自动存入「历史」，可对比两次测试的差异。

常见问题

标准版和快速版有什么区别？该选哪个？

两套题目独立命题、独立计分。标准版 60 题每个维度覆盖更充分，结果更稳定；快速版 24 题只取核心题目，速度快但边界类型的判定可能摆动。

建议：第一次做标准版；想快速看看倾向或重测验证一致性时用快速版。两次结果不同是正常的，看“偏向强度”比看四个字母更有意义。

结果会不会不准？

性格测评本质是自我报告的倾向测量，受当时的情绪、状态和答题心态影响，同一个人隔一段时间测出不同结果很常见。

把它当作自我观察的起点而不是结论：重点看哪些描述让你“被说中”，哪些不像你——不像的部分往往更值得想。

换手机 / 删了 App，历史记录还在吗？

不在。本应用不设账号、数据不上传，历史记录只存在本机。删除 App 或更换设备后记录即丢失，无法恢复。

建议在结果页截图保存，或先看完再换设备。

为什么答题结束后会出现全屏广告？

应用免费提供全部题目与解读，靠广告维持运营。插页广告只在每次测试完成、点击「查看结果」时出现一次，不会打断答题过程。

关闭或跳过广告后即进入结果页；若当时没有可用的广告（网络不稳或无填充），会直接进入结果页，不会被卡住。

广告加载不出来 / 一直是空白？

广告需要联网获取，网络不稳、开启了广告拦截或在部分地区时可能没有可用广告。这不影响任何功能，直接继续使用即可。

如果广告位长期空白并影响体验，请告诉我们你的地区和网络类型。

结果页的「深度解读」解锁不了？

该部分通过观看一段激励视频解锁。若视频加载失败，通常是网络问题——切换网络后重试即可。全部题目与基础解读不受影响，始终免费。

可以离线使用吗？

可以。答题、计分、类型解读、历史记录全部在本机完成，飞行模式下也能正常测试与查看结果；只有广告需要联网。

PersonaAtlas · 16 Personality Test · Guide & FAQ

Based on Jungian type theory, the app places you among the 16 personality types using either the 60-question standard test or the 24-question quick test, then shows how strongly you lean on each of four dimensions, a full type profile, and notes on working with other types.

Everything you answer and every result stays on your device. No sign-up, no login, and no server.

Getting started

  1. Pick a length. Start with the standard 60-question test (about 8 minutes) for a steadier result; the 24-question quick test (about 3 minutes) is there when time is short.
  2. Answer question by question. Go with your first instinct and don't overthink any single item — and don't answer as the person you wish you were. If you leave mid-test, the unfinished part is not saved.
  3. See your result. You get your four-letter type, per-dimension percentages, and the full profile right away.
  4. Read deeper. Open the detail page for your type, or browse all 16 types any time from the Type Library on the home screen.
  5. Look back. Every result is saved to History so you can compare two runs.

Frequently asked

What is the difference between the two lengths? Which should I take?

They are separate question sets with separate scoring. The 60-question version covers each dimension more thoroughly and gives a steadier result; the 24-question version keeps only the core items, so borderline types can shift.

Take the standard test first. Use the quick test when you want a fast read or want to check consistency. Two different results are normal — the strength of your leaning matters more than the four letters.

Can the result be wrong?

This is a self-report measure of preference, so mood, context, and how you approach the questions all affect it. Getting a different type at a different time is common.

Treat it as a starting point for self-observation, not a verdict. Notice which lines land and which don't — the ones that don't are often the more interesting material.

Will my history survive a new phone or reinstalling the app?

No. There is no account and nothing is uploaded, so history lives only on this device. Deleting the app or switching devices removes it permanently.

Screenshot your result if you want to keep it.

Why does a full-screen ad appear after I finish?

The app is free and ad-supported. An interstitial appears once per completed test, when you tap "See result" — it never interrupts the questions themselves.

Close or skip it and you go straight to your result. If no ad is available (weak network or no fill), you are taken to the result immediately and are never blocked.

Ads won't load, or the space stays blank

Ads require a network connection. On unstable connections, with a content blocker, or in some regions there may be no ad to show. No feature depends on it — just keep going.

If the slot is blank for a long stretch, tell us your region and connection type.

I can't unlock the extended reading

That section unlocks by watching a rewarded video. If it fails to load, it is usually the connection — switch networks and retry. All questions and the core reading stay free regardless.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Questions, scoring, type profiles, and history are all handled on-device and work in airplane mode. Only ads need a connection.

The app follows your system language: Simplified Chinese system language shows Chinese, anything else shows English. Change the system language and reopen the app.

Your call — nothing in the app depends on it. If you decline you still see ads, but they will be less relevant and more repetitive. Allowing it makes ads more relevant to your interests.

You can change it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Do you collect my answers?

No. Your answers and results are written only to local storage. They are never uploaded or tied to your identity. See the Privacy Policy for details.

If something goes wrong

Try these first — they resolve most issues:

  • Make sure the device is on iOS 16 or later;
  • Force-quit the app and reopen it;
  • Switch between Wi-Fi and cellular if the connection is flaky;
  • Reinstall as a last resort (note: this clears your history).
